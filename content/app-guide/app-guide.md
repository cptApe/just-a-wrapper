# App guide

This app is a workspace where you hand work to a persistent AI agent. You describe what you want in plain language; the agent does it, saves the results, and can keep doing it on a schedule. This guide describes every page and capability in the app, written for the people who use it.

## Home (New task)

The home page is one big composer. Type what you want done and press enter; that starts a task and takes you to its thread.

- The tier picker (top left) chooses the agent: Fast for quick answers, Balanced for most work, Expert for the hardest jobs. Higher tiers use more credits.
- Attach files by dragging them anywhere onto the page, or pick files and folders from your Library with the attach button.
- You can also pick skills to guide the task, and record voice input instead of typing.
- The credits pill (top right) shows what is left on your plan and opens billing shortcuts.

## Tasks

Every conversation is a task. Recent tasks live in the sidebar; pin the important ones and they stay at the top.

- Inside a task you chat with the agent, watch it work step by step, and approve actions when it asks.
- Files the agent produces are collected behind the files button in the task header and saved to your Library.
- Share a task with the share button: keep it private or create a public link.
- Rename, pin, or delete a task from its row menu in the sidebar.
- If a task runs out of credits it pauses and resumes after a top up.

## Scheduled

Work that runs on its own. The page has three groups:

- Requires your attention: tasks waiting on you, for example a paused task or one that needs an approval.
- Upcoming: standing schedules and event triggers, with their next run time. Pause, resume, run now, or delete from the row menu.
- History: past runs, each one openable as a normal task thread.

Create one with New schedule: give it a title, a prompt, and either a repeating schedule (daily, weekly, whatever you need) or an event trigger from a connected app (for example, a new email).

## Tools

Three tabs: Apps, Skills, and Built-in.

- Apps are connections to the services you already use: Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Calendly, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, OneDrive, Dropbox, Notion, Stripe, Square, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Mailchimp, and Canva. Open an app and press Connect; after a short sign-in the agent can work with your real data there. Your connections are personal to you. A workspace owner or admin can also share one account per app from the app's Accounts tab. Your agent uses your own account when you have one, and the shared account when you do not.
- Skills are reusable know-how for the agent: house style, repeatable processes, checklists. Presets come built in; create your own with New skill, where the agent interviews you and writes the skill for you.
- Built-in lists the agent's own abilities (like browsing and file work) and lets you set which ones always need your approval.

## Library

Your workspace's files and the agent's memory, in one place.

- Files: everything you upload and everything the agent produces, organized in folders. Upload, create folders, write notes, rename, move, share a folder with the workspace, and restore recently deleted items from the trash.
- Memory: what the agent remembers between sessions. Two scopes: You (personal) and Workspace (shared, edited by admins). Read, edit, and browse notes, and restore older versions from history.

Anything you tell the agent to remember lands here and shapes future tasks.

## Search

Press Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows) anywhere, or use Search in the sidebar. One box finds your tasks, library files and folders, memory notes, apps, skills, scheduled tasks, and settings pages, and offers quick actions like New task or New schedule. If nothing matches, hand the question to the agent from the same menu.

## Notifications

The bell in the sidebar. The agent notifies you when a task finishes, needs an approval, or runs into trouble. Tune what you get, and whether email is included, in Settings, Notifications.

## Settings

- General: workspace basics like name and appearance.
- Account: your profile, email, and sign-in.
- Members: invite teammates and manage roles. Owners and admins administer the workspace.
- Billing: your plan, credit balance, top ups, and invoices.
- Usage: an activity log of what the agent did and what it cost.
- Notifications: which alerts you receive and where.
- Workspace: rename the workspace or delete it. You can also create and switch workspaces from the sidebar's workspace menu.

## Good to know

- Credits: every task consumes credits based on the work done. The balance lives in Billing and on the home page.
- Permissions: the agent asks before an app action that changes something, unless you set that action to Allow in the app's Actions tab.
- Workspaces: each workspace has its own tasks, library, memory, connections, and members. Switch or create workspaces from the sidebar.
- Sharing: tasks can be shared by link; library folders can be shared with the workspace.
